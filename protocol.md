# CRISPR Knockout Screening Protocol

**Status:** Under review

## Overview
Protocol for generating single-gene knockouts in HEK293 cells using
CRISPR-Cas9 ribonucleoprotein (RNP) delivery, for downstream phenotypic
screening.

## Reagents
- Cas9 protein (NLS-tagged)
- Synthetic sgRNA (target-specific)
- Electroporation buffer
- HEK293 cells, passage < 20
- Positive control sgRNA.
- Non-targeting control sgRNA

## Steps
1. Design and order sgRNA targeting exon 2 of the gene of interest.
2. Complex Cas9 protein with sgRNA at a 1:1.2 molar ratio, incubate 10 min RT.
3. Electroporate RNP complex into 2x10^5 cells per reaction.
4. Recover cells in antibiotic-free media for 48-72h.
5. Validate editing efficiency by amplicon sequencing.
